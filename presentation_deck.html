The Problem & Campus Context

The Disconnected Campus Reality

Isolated Data Silos

Attendance in biometric systems, marks in exam cells, assignments in Moodle LMS, and mock test scores in the Placement Cell never talk to each other.

Lagging / Reactive Alerts

Faculty discover students are failing only after semester end-exams, or that students cannot clear corporate coding rounds only during Day-1 placement drives.

One-Size-Fits-All Approach

High-CGPA students with weak soft skills need completely different interventions than low-attendance students with backlogs, yet colleges treat them uniformly.

The Mission: Move beyond generic dashboards. Build an actionable decision intelligence platform that integrates 7 streams, computes an explainable Student Success Score, and prescribes targeted interventions.

Core Deliverable 2

The Student Success Score (3S Index)

Formal Multi-Criteria Composite Index (0 to 100 Scale):

3S = 0.30 × S_Academic + 0.20 × S_Placement + 0.20 × S_Engagement + 0.15 × S_Attendance + 0.15 × S_LMS
Tier 1: High Achievers 3S ≥ 80.0

147 students. Nominate for Tier-1 Product MNCs, research fellowships, and leadership.

Tier 2: Good Standing 65.0 ≤ 3S < 80.0

757 students. Stable cohort. Guide into specialized domain electives and certifications.

Tier 3: Moderate Risk 50.0 ≤ 3S < 65.0

476 students. Vulnerable in 1-2 pillars. Targeted mentor coaching and aptitude drilling.

Tier 4: Critical Attention 3S < 50.0

120 students. Immediate intervention: Dean review, condonation warning, Saturday backlog clinics.

Bonus Feature 2 & Decision Intelligence

Explainability & What-If Simulation

Explainable Score (XAI)

Eliminates black-box ambiguity. Decomposes each student's score relative to campus population norms:

Positive Catalysts (+): Identifies protective factors (e.g. +2.8 from high CGPA, +1.9 from NPTEL certifications).
Negative Drags (-): Flags specific drivers pulling down the score (e.g. -3.4 from 2 active backlogs, -2.1 from low coding score).
"What-If" Scenario Simulator

Administrators and mentors can simulate interventions dynamically:

Attendance: +10% LMS Completion: +15% Clear Backlogs: Yes
Result: 3S Score rises from 48.2 → 63.5 (+15.3 pts), Academic Risk drops from 88% → 12%!
